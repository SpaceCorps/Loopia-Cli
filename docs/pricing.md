## Loopia Platform & Registrar Services
The CLI interacts with the official Loopia registrar and hosting service ([loopia.se](https://www.loopia.se)). Domain registration, DNS management, and hosting fees are billed directly by Loopia AB:
- **Domain Registrations**: Standard TLD renewal and registration fees (e.g. .se, .nu, .com).
- **DNS Services**: LoopiaDNS or domain-bundled zone management.
- **Web Hosting**: Starter, Hosting Private, Hosting Business, or Business Plus tiers.
- **PrePAID Credits**: Account balance for domain renewals and invoice payments.

For official pricing and TLD registration schedules, visit [https://www.loopia.se](https://www.loopia.se).
